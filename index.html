Random Field Shapes

Each tile is one seed. The seed grows a random graph of operators over 3D space, and the shape is wherever that field is negative, cut off by a cube. A second random field picks the colour. Some graphs also include a simulated field: a random local rule run for a few dozen steps on a 64-cell grid. Open a tile to rotate it and read the formula it grew.
